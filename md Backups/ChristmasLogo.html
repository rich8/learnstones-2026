Add the filled "lime [] around the tree part to make it green, (or lime so you can see whats happening - btw. penup doesn't work inside the filled shape)

cs setpensize 1 filled "yellow [repeat 5 [fd 30 rt 144]] setpencolor "green setpensize 1 filled "lime [ rt 150 repeat 3 [fd 20 rt 120] lt 150 bk 10 rt 150 repeat 3 [fd 30 rt 120] lt 150 bk 15 rt 150 repeat 3 [fd 40 rt 120] lt 150 bk 20 rt 150 repeat 3 [fd 50 rt 120] lt 150 bk 25 rt 150 repeat 3 [fd 60 rt 120] lt 150 bk 30 ]

Use a subroutine with the size variable to reduce repetition and increase flexibility (eg. change the overlapping fraction 0.5)

to tri :size rt 150 repeat 3 [fd :size rt 120] lt 150 pu bk (:size * 0.5) pd END cs setpensize 1 filled "yellow [repeat 5 [fd 30 rt 144]] setpencolor "green setpensize 1 filled "lime [ tri 20 tri 30 tri 40 tri 50 tri 60 ] bk 40

For Loop ... for [var start stop step][ statements to repeat]

The For loop is similar to using repeat with repcount but is a little more elegant. Now you easily change the number and size of the segments

to tri :size rt 150 repeat 3 [fd :size rt 120] lt 150 pu bk (:size * 0.5) pd END cs setpensize 1 filled "yellow [repeat 5 [fd 30 rt 144]] setpencolor "green setpensize 1 filled "lime [ for [sg 20 60 10][tri :sg] ] bk 40
